01.

About

I'm a Computer Engineering student with a strong interest in Linux, open-source software, and systems engineering. I enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface — not just writing code, but exploring the architecture, tooling, and decisions that make software reliable and maintainable.

During Google Summer of Code 2025, I contributed to FOSSology, the Linux Foundation's license-compliance toolkit: I packaged 29 missing PHP dependencies for Debian, migrated build systems to CMake, and verified reproducible builds in Docker — working closely with Debian maintainers and the FOSSology community.

Right now I'm gaining industry experience as an IT Intern at GUPCO (Gulf of Suez Petroleum), learning how enterprise IT supports large-scale operations — and sharpening my backend skills with REST APIs, Spring Boot, and scalable system design on the side.

When I'm not coding, I'm teaching: I record Arabic playlists that break down compiler design and computer organization for students like me.
